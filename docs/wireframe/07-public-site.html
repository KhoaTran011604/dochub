Occupational Safety Training Minutes — Q3 2026

Last updated: 28 Sep, 2026

On September 15, 2026, Factory A held its quarterly occupational safety training session for all production staff. The session focused on safe machine operation procedures, use of personal protective equipment, and emergency response procedures.

Training Content

  • Safety regulations for operating stamping and cutting machines
  • Guidelines for using and storing personal protective equipment (PPE)
  • First aid and incident reporting procedures
  • Fire and explosion evacuation drills

A total of 86 out of 90 staff attended in full. The 4 excused absences were scheduled for makeup training on September 22, 2026. The end-of-session assessment showed a 97% pass rate among staff.

Assessment and Recommendations

The Labor Safety Committee rated the session as highly effective and recommends maintaining the quarterly training schedule, with the addition of chemical safety content in the upcoming Q4 training.
